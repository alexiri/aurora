Theme switch

Lives in the account menu and in Account › Profile. It follows the system by default and is remembered per browser.

Rules

  • Never pure black; never pure white text on large areas.
  • No shadows: elevation is surface lightness plus a 1px line.
  • Primary inverts to light ink; Approve is bright teal with dark text.
  • The logo keeps its glow strokes; its tile becomes subtle.
  • Agent avatars and images keep their content; only tints change.
