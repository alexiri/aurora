Lockups, app icons and mono marks

One mark, both themes

The twisted teal ribbon is the same in light and dark. The star, wordmark and tagline switch: ink, ink and muted on light; #E8ECF3, #E8ECF3 and #B4BDCC on dark.

The twist

One ribbon: the deep-teal leg passes under the mint band at the apex. Keep the fold edge, pointed inner apex and flared feet; never redraw it as two strokes.

Wordmark

"AURORA" is lettered and supplied as outlines; never retype it in a font. The tagline belongs only to the stacked lockup and the sign-in panel.

Small sizes

Below 24 px use the mono mark (a gap keeps the fold readable). The favicon swaps the star colour with the system theme.
