# FIX-236: emergency read access for organization owners (owner 2026-11-23, security fix)

## Why

FIX-234 (D-477) removes the implicit owner role, so no organization role can read a workspace's conversations any
more. Freezing, suspending and deleting stop harm without reading anything. Legal and incident work sometimes needs the
content, though. The owner, 2026-11-23, answering the coordinator: "Break-glass is org-owner-only."

## Decision, contract, security

- **D-478** (`docs/90-decisions.md`):
  - (a) Only an org owner opens it, in a session, for one workspace, with a reason, for 1–24 hours (default 4).
  - (b) It is read-only and covers what an owner reads.
  - (c) The workspace's people and the org's other owners are told at once, and the workspace shows a banner.
  - (d) Every conversation read is recorded before content is returned. It cannot be extended.
- **SEC-154** (`docs/17-security.md`): its invariant and proving tests are this brief's security acceptance.
- **Contracts.** The lane writes these in `docs/API_CONTRACTS.md` after FIX-234's C-1163, plus their `05` and `03`
  rows.

**C-1164 Emergency access rows and routes.**
- **Table `workspace_emergency_access`** (one migration, the next free number):
  - `id`; `org_id`; `workspace_id` (FK, cascade); `user_id` (FK→users, cascade);
  - `reason text` (10–500 characters, sanitized);
  - `opened_at`; `expires_at` (`opened_at + hours`, 1–24); `ended_at null`;
  - `ended_by uuid null` (FK→users, set null); `end_reason text null` (CHECK in `ended`, `expired`, `access_lost`).
  - A partial unique index on (`workspace_id`, `user_id`) `WHERE ended_at IS NULL`.
  - **Active** means `ended_at IS NULL AND expires_at > now()`, always evaluated at use.
  - Retention: rows are kept 400 days, then the nightly sweep deletes them. Audit rows stay under the audit's own
    retention.
- **`POST /v1/workspaces/{ws}/emergency-access` `{ reason, hours? }`** → 201 `EmergencyAccess`.
  - Who: a session (API key: 403 `session_required`) of an active **effective org owner** of the workspace's
    organization.
    - An org admin, a member and an instance admin who is not an org owner get 403 `org_owner_required`.
    - A caller who already has own `can_own` there gets 409 `already_has_access`.
    - A caller with one already active gets 409 `emergency_access_active`.
  - Validation: 422 on `/reason` or `/hours`.
  - Rate: class `invite`.
  - The audit row `workspace.emergency_access.open` is security-critical, written in the transaction, with the reason
    and hours.
  - Then the notifications (C-1165).
- **`POST /v1/emergency-access/{id}/end`**:
  - Who: the opener, or any active org owner of the organization.
  - Effect: `ended_at = now()`, `end_reason = ended`, critical audit row `workspace.emergency_access.end`.
  - It is idempotent: ending one that already ended answers 200 with the row.
- **`GET /v1/workspaces/{ws}/emergency-access`** (any caller with own access, or with `can_administer`) and **`GET
  /v1/orgs/{org}/emergency-access`** (org owners and admins): the rows, newest first, paged.
- **`EmergencyAccess`** = `{ id, workspace: { id, name }, by: { id, display_name }, reason, opened_at, expires_at,
  ended_at, end_reason, active }`.
- **Expiry.** A background job, alongside the existing sweeps, every minute, sets `ended_at = expires_at` and
  `end_reason = expired` and writes `workspace.emergency_access.expire`. Use never waits for the job: every check reads
  `expires_at > now()` itself.
- If the opener stops being an active org owner (role lowered, suspended, removed), the row ends with `access_lost` in
  the same transaction as that change.

**C-1165 What emergency access lets someone read, and what everyone else sees.**
- **One helper.** `authz::emergency_reader(exec, user, ws) -> Option<EmergencyGrant>` checks the active row **and**
  that the user is still an active effective org owner.
- **A separate read type.** Read routes take `ReadAuth = Own(WorkspaceAuth) | Emergency(EmergencyGrant)` through a new
  `Caller::workspace_read(ws)`.
  - `EmergencyGrant` can never become a `WorkspaceAuth` or an `AdminAuth`: no `From`, no constructor outside `authz`.
  - So `can_use`, `can_approve`, `can_own`, `can_administer` and `can_ssh`, and every write, never accept it.
- **The routes that take `ReadAuth`, and no others.** Generate the list from the router and record it in the contract:
  - the workspace (`GET /v1/workspaces/{ws}`);
  - its agents list and agent detail;
  - the conversation lists and folders;
  - `GET /v1/conversations/{id}`, its events, timeline and read stream (SSE);
  - attachment download;
  - export (`?attachments=none|zip`);
  - search within the workspace;
  - activity (the request log);
  - each agent's memory (list and read), schedules and webhooks (list and detail, including `schedule_runs` and
    `webhook_deliveries`), and skills (list and read);
  - the workspace's people (`GET …/access`).

  Through `can_see_conversation`, the emergency reader sees every conversation of the workspace, direct ones included.
- **Never with emergency access:**
  - posting, uploading, abort, send-now, steer, follow-up actions;
  - archive, delete, redact;
  - approvals (feed and decide);
  - terminal, Files, SSH certificate, preview, relay;
  - grants, connections, secrets, policy;
  - evals; any PATCH, PUT or DELETE.
- **Recording.**
  - Every response that returns a conversation's **content** writes `conversation.emergency_view` (actor, conversation,
    emergency access id) **in the same transaction or before the body is sent**. That covers its events, timeline,
    stream open, an attachment and the export. If the write fails, the read is refused (503), so this is not
    best-effort like `conversation.owner_view`.
  - Rows are deduplicated: once per (actor, conversation, hour).
  - An export writes `conversation.export` with `via: emergency` as well.
- **Notifications at open.** Recipients:
  - every active person in `own_workspace_access` of the workspace (for a personal workspace, that is its person);
  - every other active org owner;
  - never the opener.

  Channels: e-mail and Web Push, through FIX-167's path. Subject "Emergency read access to <Workspace>". Body: "<Name>,
  an owner of <Org>, opened read access to <Workspace>'s conversations until <time, recipient's timezone>. Reason:
  <reason>. Everything they open is recorded." It links to the workspace.

  Ending or expiring sends nothing more; the banner covers it.
- **`Workspace` additions** (C-1162's `audience` and the DTO):
  - `audience.emergency: { id, by: { id, display_name }, reason, opened_at, expires_at, ended_at, active } | null`.
    This is the newest row that is active or ended within the last 7 days. Every caller who gets `audience` sees it.
  - `emergency_reader: boolean`: true while the caller holds an active one.
- **Instructions.** `10 §Visibility of direct conversations`, the agent preamble: no change. The agent's instructions
  do not mention emergency access.

## Change

**DB:** the migration and table (`03`: move the row out of `§Planned changes`), `authz::emergency_reader`, a repo
module `emergency_access` (open, end, list, expire, end-on-lost-ownership), and the `membership_queries` guard counts.

**API:**
1. The routes (C-1164), the expiry job, and the access-lost hook in the org member role, suspend and remove writers
   (`tenancy/service.rs`, the org members routes).
2. `Caller::workspace_read` and `ReadAuth`. Switch **only** the C-1165 routes to it. The conversation helpers
   (`conversations/service.rs` `load_conversation_auth`, `read_conversation`, `attachments/service.rs`,
   `lifecycle.rs` export, `timeline/feed.rs`, `repo/search.rs`) take `ReadAuth` for reads and keep `WorkspaceAuth` for
   writes.
3. Recording and notifications (C-1165). Add a new notify kind to FIX-167's sender.
4. `Workspace.audience.emergency` and `emergency_reader`. Regenerate OpenAPI and `schema.d.ts`.
5. **Route inventory.** FIX-234's test gains a third list, the `ReadAuth` routes, and fails on any route in two lists or
   in none.

**Web (`web/src`):**
1. **Opening it.** On FIX-235's manage page (`/o/<org>/workspaces/<ws>`), for org owners only, add "Open emergency read
   access".
   - The dialog asks for a reason (textarea, 10–500 characters) and a duration (select: 1, 2, 4 (default), 8 or 24
     hours).
   - Then the confirmation ladder's danger step: "Everyone in <Workspace> and the organization's other owners will be
     told, with your reason. Everything you open is recorded. You can read, not act."
   - On success, go to the workspace.
2. **Emergency mode.** When `emergency_reader` is true, the workspace shell renders read-only:
   - **Shown:** Chat (every conversation, direct ones included, in the list, with no composer, no Stop and no "…"
     menus), Activity, each agent's Settings (read-only), Memory, Automations (lists), Skills, People.
   - **Hidden:** Terminal, Files, Computer, previews, Apps & access, approvals, every save bar and every action button.
   - **A banner on every page:** "Emergency read access until 18:00. Everything you open is recorded, and this
     workspace's people have been told." It has an "End now" button.
   - Use the existing read-only owner view's components wherever they fit.
   - 390 px and both themes, from shared primitives only.
3. **The workspace's banner for its people** while `audience.emergency` is set: "<Name>, an organization owner, has
   emergency read access to this workspace until 18:00. Reason: <reason>." After it ends: "…had emergency read access
   from 14:00 to 18:00…". Shown on every page of the workspace for 7 days, and dismissible per person (local storage,
   with try/catch).
4. **The chat line.** `lib/chat/audience.ts` `visibilityLines` appends, while it is active: "<Name>, an organization
   owner, can read this workspace's conversations until 18:00 (emergency access)." (D-479).
5. **History.** Add an "Emergency access" section to the workspace's People page and to the organization's audit
   topic, from the two GET routes, showing who, reason, from–to and how it ended.

## Tests

The SEC-154 proving tests, plus:
- **Open:**
  - an org owner opens it with a reason and 4 hours → 201, a critical audit row, and e-mail and push to Bob (the
    person) and to the other owner, not to the opener (mock senders);
  - an org admin → 403 `org_owner_required`; an instance admin who is not an org owner → 403;
  - an API key → 403 `session_required`; reason under 10 characters or `hours: 25` → 422;
  - a second open → 409; an owner with own access → 409 `already_has_access`.
- **Reading Bob's direct conversation, with recording:**
  - it works and writes `conversation.emergency_view` once per hour;
  - an injected audit failure → 503 and no body;
  - the export writes both rows;
  - an attachment download works.
- **Refused during it** (one table test over the inventory's non-`ReadAuth` workspace routes): every workspace write,
  posting, abort, approvals decide, terminal/files/SSH/preview tickets, grants. The gateway's `can_use` stays false.
- **Ending:**
  - with the clock past `expires_at` and before the sweep: reads refused;
  - the sweep writes `expire`;
  - `end` by the opener or another owner works, and by a member is 403;
  - lowering the opener's org role ends it with `access_lost`.
- **`Workspace.audience.emergency`:**
  - set while active and for 7 days after, null after that;
  - `emergency_reader` is true only for the opener while it is active.
- **Web:**
  - unit tests for the extra visibility line and for the banners (active, ended);
  - emergency mode hides every action (a list assertion over the shell's buttons);
  - the open dialog's validation and danger step;
  - Playwright mocked: an owner opens it from the manage page, reads a direct chat with no composer, and ends it; a
    member's workspace shows the banner; check 390 px.

## Acceptance

- An org owner can open time-limited, read-only access to one workspace with a reason, from the manage page.
- The workspace's people and the other owners are told at once, and the workspace shows the banner.
- Every conversation read is recorded or refused, and nothing can be changed or started.
- It ends on time without the sweep, can be ended early, and ends when the opener stops being an owner.
- The SEC-154 tests and the route inventory pass.
- Docs and contracts: C-1164, C-1165, `03`, `04`, `05`, `10`, `14`, `17`, `security-coverage.md`.
- Clean: fmt, clippy, the ITs named above, `extract-schema --check`, `guards/run-all.sh`, web vitest/tsc/lint/build,
  the touched Playwright specs.

Size **M–L**. Scope: Rust (API, DB, one migration) and Next.js.
- **Depends on FIX-234 and FIX-235** (`can_administer`, `audience`, the manage page, `visibilityLines`, the route
  inventory).
- **Security review:** before merge, an Opus review against SEC-154.
