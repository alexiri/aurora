# FIX-206: Members and approvers see no "Set for you by …" limits they cannot change (owner 2026-11-21)

## Why

D-442 (P5-LAYOUT-5) stopped drawing the workspace BYO limit for people who can neither change nor tighten it, and asked
the owner whether the rule should reach the other ladders. The owner, 2026-11-21: **"Yes, hide them for members and
approvers."** Recorded as **D-444**.

Today a viewer who cannot edit Internet access (a workspace member or approver; an organization member on the
organization's page) gets, under the summary, "Your rules" and a who-to-ask notice, a section **Limits** with a
`ScopeLadder` of locked rows ("Set for you by an Aurora administrator", "Set for you by <org>"), each with who can change
it (`components/internet/internet-level.tsx`, the `!p.canEdit` branch, ~:337–379, `<Section id="internet-limits"
title="Limits"><ScopeLadder above={limitsFromAbove("workspace", p.eff, who)} /></Section>`). Nothing in it can be acted on.

## Decision

**D-444** (owner, 2026-11-21; extends D-442(c)): a viewer who can change nothing at a level (a workspace member or
approver; an organization member) is not shown the limits set above that level. The page still shows what is in force
for them in words (the summary sentence, the published rules as text) and one who-to-ask line for this level. Owners and
admins keep the ladder where **Restrict further…** offers a stricter choice; where it offers none, its rows are not drawn
either (as D-442 does for BYO). Amends `14 §Scope ladder` and D-406's "a plain member at Use reads the limits from above".

## Change (web only; no API or contract change)

1. **Internet access, non-editors** (`internet-level.tsx`, `!p.canEdit` branch, both levels): remove the **Limits**
   section. Keep the description/summary, the `WhoCan` notice and "Your rules". The `limitsFromAbove` read for this branch
   goes if nothing else needs it (no extra request for members).
2. **Every other place a non-editor sees a "Set for you by …" row on a workspace or organization page**: find them with
   `rg -n "ScopeLadder|<Locked|Set for you by|LockPill owner" web/src` and check each page's non-editor branch (expected:
   none left after 1 and P5-LAYOUT-5; organization AI models/agents pages are for owners and admins). List what you found
   and what you did in the report.
3. **Owners with nothing to tighten:** in a `collapsed` ladder (a workspace owner's view), when the "here" controls offer
   no stricter value for any row, the ladder draws nothing; let callers say so with a prop (e.g. `canTighten={false}`)
   rather than guessing inside `ScopeLadder`. Internet access almost always has something to tighten: no visible change
   expected there; the prop exists for the rule and is unit-tested.
4. **docs/14**: §Scope ladder: "**Above** is drawn only for viewers who can change this level and only when something can
   still be tightened (D-442, D-444); a viewer who can change nothing reads what is in force in words and who to ask." Fix
   §Altitudes / D-406 wording that says members read the limits.
