# FIX-210: The App step's search and tabs sit above the list, not floating over it (owner 2026-11-21)

## Why

The owner, in **Give Daneel an app**: "when you start scrolling apps the two toggle switches are stuck in place and apps
scroll underneath, which is really weird." The screenshot shows the **Ready · 13 / Needs an admin · 7** tabs pinned
mid-air with the tiles sliding behind them, the "READY TO CONNECT · 13" heading cut in half above them, and the search
box gone.

Cause: `web/src/components/apps/connect-journey/app-step.tsx:152` wraps the tabs in `sticky top-0 z-10 -mx-1 bg-surface
px-1 pb-1` (P5-REFINE-13, access-7: "the tabs never scroll away"). The sticky element lives **inside** the dialog's
scrolling body (`components/ui/dialog.tsx:123`, `overflow-y-auto px-6 py-4`), so it sticks to the body's top edge while
the search box above it and the body's top padding scroll away, and the list shows through the padding above the pill.

## Change (web only; no API, contract or decision change: access-7's "never scroll away" still holds)

1. **Search and tabs leave the scrolling body.** On the App step, the search field and the two tabs render in the
   dialog's fixed header area, under the stepper (`DialogContent`'s `header` slot, which never scrolls, D-393). Only the
   tab panel (the groups and tiles) scrolls in the body. No `sticky` anywhere in the step.
   - How: either lift the step's `query` and `tab` state into the journey and pass the controls as part of `stepper`/
     `header` for the App step only, or give `AppStep` a header target like `useFooterSlot` (`components/credentials/
     llm-form.tsx:107`) does for footers (a `useHeaderSlot` next to it, rendered by `JourneyFrame` under the stepper).
     Pick the one that keeps both journeys simple and say which.
   - The tabs keep `role="tablist"`, their labels with counts, and the tabpanel's `aria-labelledby`; the search keeps
     `aria-label="Search apps"`; focus order: search → tabs → the first tile.
2. **Both journeys**: the workspace journey (`connect-journey.tsx`) and the organization journey (`org-journey.tsx`) use
   `AppStep`; both get the same layout.
3. **Phone (sheet, below `sm`)**: same structure; the header area must not take more than the search + tabs height; the
   list scrolls below it.
4. Other steps are unchanged (their header holds only the stepper).
