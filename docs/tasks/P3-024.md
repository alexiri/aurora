# P3-024: Self-contained `presence.deprovision` ops (RFE-024, D-67, SEC-58)

## Goal
When an agent, a workspace, an org or a channel integration is deleted, the `presence.deprovision` op is committed **in the same transaction** as the delete. It carries the sealed credentials it needs, so the connector can still remove the presence after the rows and secrets are gone. Ops are retried for 24 h, then pruned, with metrics and audit. **SEC-58** must be proven.

The wire behaviour is **C-360..C-364** in `docs/API_CONTRACTS.md` (API-P3):
- the `Operation.credentials` field;
- the new schema `connector_op_presence_deprovision.json`;
- the delete routes, including `DELETE /v1/channel-integrations/{id}` (a seal failure → 503 `secret_store_unavailable` and nothing deleted);
- results, retry and discard;
- an op whose copy can't be opened stays pending;
- the failure audit and metric.

## Scope
1. **API delete paths** (`domains/{agents,tenancy,channels}`): seal the needed credentials into the op inside the delete transaction; refuse the delete if sealing fails.
2. **Ops claim:** an op whose sealed copy can't be opened is never claimed without credentials; 24 h of retries with backoff; pruning; metrics and the failure audit.
3. **Connector SDK** (`crates/aurora-connector-sdk`) and **the Mattermost connector's** deprovision use the op's credentials, never a live lookup. Update the connector conformance suite.
4. **Migration** (additive): the `channel_ops` index. Move the `docs/03` row.
5. **Tests:** the `18` list; conformance (fake and `--mattermost`); `channels.sh`; SEC-58 → proven.
