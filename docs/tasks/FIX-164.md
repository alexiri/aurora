# FIX-164: Workspace members can read their workspace's Internet access rules (read-only)

## Goal
P5-IA-2's spec (14 §Information architecture, the Internet access page) says a workspace member sees the
workspace's Internet access read-only: its summary sentences and rules, with no Common changes, Add rule
or Tighten…, and one `WhoCan` sentence for who can change it. I-27 gates reads of workspace policy to the
workspace owner, so the merged page (`d7430c4f`) shows members only the limits from above and the
`WhoCan` sentence. **Owner, 2026-11-17 (D-365): members may read their workspace's published rules,
read-only.**

## Change
1. **D-365 amends I-27** for this one case: a member of a workspace may **read** that workspace's
   **published** Internet access policy (rules and defaults of the active generation). Never drafts,
   generation history, warnings, overrides of other workspaces, or any write. Everything else in I-27
   stays.
2. A route for it (prefer a member-readable `GET` that returns only the published rules and the
   summary inputs, not the owner's full policy document; name it in `05` and as a contract line next to
   C-964, number from PROGRESS's next-free line). Permission-matrix rows: member yes, non-member no,
   service key as today.
3. The workspace Internet access page for a member renders the summary sentences and the rules read-only
   from that route (P5-IA-2's components; no edit controls), plus the existing `WhoCan` sentence.

## Tests
ITs: a member reads the published rules; a draft is never visible; a non-member and another workspace's
member are refused; the response has no draft or history fields. vitest for the read-only page; the
Internet access Playwright spec gains the member case (both themes, 390 px, axe). `docs/tests/security-coverage.md`
row for the I-27 amendment. Small–medium (S–M); any lane, ideally with the next P5-UX web work.
