# FIX-256: suspensions survive the sign-in sync at the organization level too; FIX-261's leftovers (2026-11-27, security)

**Security review by the Orchestrator before merge (SEC-161 continued).** Runs after FIX-261 is merged (same files).

## Why

FIX-261 (D-501) made lifting a **workspace** suspension a grant on every path: restore, delete, the sign-in claim
sync, and leaving and re-joining the organization. Its lane listed what it saw but did not change
(`docs/tasks/FIX-261.md`, "What this leaves").

1. **Organization suspension is still lifted by the claim sync (medium, security).** An org membership that is
   claim-managed and **suspended** is removed by the sync's org removal pass when the claim lapses, and a returning
   claim inserts an **active** membership (`services/aurora-api/src/domains/oidc/roles.rs`, the org pass;
   `crates/aurora-db/src/repo/members.rs`). So a suspended member comes back as an active member just by having the
   identity provider drop and re-add their group. That is the same bypass D-501 closed for workspace bindings.
2. **A suspended binding whose claim lapsed stays claim-managed (low).** `DELETE` then answers 409
   `managed_by_idp`, and a restore gives the lapsed claim's role until the person's next sign-in.
3. **`PATCH` on a departed member's kept row commits, then answers 404 (low).** FIX-261 keeps a suspended binding
   when a person leaves. The row is hidden, but an administrator who knows the id can still `PATCH` it. The change
   commits, and the answer, which reads active members only, is 404. The answer and the effect disagree.
4. **Recorded, not changed:**
   - a stronger claim still raises the role stored on a suspended row (no access until someone restores it);
   - a suspension doesn't follow a person to a **new user row** (account deleted and recreated, or a SCIM link moved);
   - account deletion's "sole personal workspace" check counts a kept row.

   Say each in `docs/04` §suspension as current behaviour. Nothing to build.

## Decision

**D-492** (Orchestrator, 2026-11-27; extends D-501 to the organization level; the owner may reverse it): a suspension,
of a workspace binding **or of an organization membership**, is lifted only by a person through the API's rules,
never by the identity provider's sync.
- When the claim behind a **suspended** org membership lapses, the sync leaves the membership in place and suspended,
  and logs it with ids only.
- A returning claim does not reactivate a suspended membership. It may update the claim-managed role, but the status
  stays `suspended`.
- An active claim-managed membership keeps today's behaviour: removed when the claim lapses, inserted when it
  returns.
- SCIM deprovisioning: check that it already follows this (FIX-261 changed `scim/deprovision.rs`). If it doesn't,
  apply the same rule and say so.

## Change

1. **Org pass of the claim sync** (`oidc/roles.rs`; `repo/members.rs` / `repo/idp_roles.rs` as needed): implement
   D-492 as above. Keep the lock order FIX-261 uses.
2. **A lapsed claim on a suspended binding becomes a manual row.** When the sync finds a suspended binding whose
   claim lapsed (FIX-261 now skips it), it **clears the claim management** of that row (the `idp_*` columns, back to
   its base role). The row is then an ordinary manual suspended binding: `DELETE` and restore follow D-501, and a
   restore gives its base role. Do the same for an org membership under D-492, if the schema has the same columns.
   No migration, if the existing columns allow it; if one is needed, take the next free number when placed.
3. **Departed members' rows aren't addressable.** `PATCH` and `DELETE` of a binding whose person is no longer an
   active member of the org answer 404 **before** any change, with the same body as an unknown binding. The kept
   row stays as FIX-261 left it.
4. **Docs:** D-492 in `docs/90-decisions.md` (this text), `docs/04` §suspension (the organization rule, item 4's
   current behaviours), `docs/17` SEC-161 (one sentence), `API_CONTRACTS` where FIX-261 amended C-1161.

## Tests (each must fail before the fix)

- A claim-managed, org-suspended member's claim lapses, then returns. Still a suspended member, and no workspace
  access returns.
- An active claim-managed member's claim lapses: removed, as today.
- A suspended binding's claim lapses. The row is now manual: `DELETE` follows D-501 (no 409), and a restore gives
  the base role.
- `PATCH` and `DELETE` on a departed member's kept row: 404, and the row is unchanged afterwards.
- Run the whole `aurora-db` and `aurora-api` sets through `scripts/dev/test.sh`, plus `oidc_roles`, `org_role_admin`
  and `authz_groups` first.

## Acceptance

1. No sync path lifts or lowers a suspension at either level.
2. A lapsed claim on a suspended row leaves an ordinary manual suspended row.
3. A departed member's row is unaddressable (404, unchanged).
4. Docs as above; fmt check by exit code, clippy, guards and `extract-schema --check` pass.

### Security notes (for the SEC-161 review)
- **Race closed in `delete_user`** (found while re-reading the diff, not in the brief): the membership was read
  unlocked, so an administrator's suspension committing before the delete was removed with it.
- **Departed check:** the membership is read `FOR SHARE` after the binding lock, so it cannot leave `active` before
  the change commits. No test forces that interleaving.
- **Possible deadlock, no integrity effect:** that order is binding → membership. Removing an org **owner** locks the
  membership first, and so does SCIM `DELETE` now. Either one racing a `PATCH`/`DELETE` of the same person's binding
  can make Postgres abort one transaction (an error answer, nothing half-written). Not reproduced.
- **A moved SCIM link (not changed; the brief's item 4):** `move_link` still removes the old account's membership,
  suspended or not. From the code, not tested: moving a still-unbound placeholder away and back would insert an
  **active** membership for it. Keeping that row would be a small follow-up if the review wants it.
- **Org-suspended people:** their bindings can no longer be removed (or reassigned with `reassign_to`) through the
  workspace route until they are active again; removing the member, or `reassign_to` on the member `PATCH`, remain.
- **The kept org membership** is listed under Members as suspended; an administrator may restore or remove it (a
  person lifting it). Restoring it leaves the SCIM link `removed` until the directory `POST`s the person again.
- **Returning claim:** the sync writes nothing to a suspended org membership (unchanged), so its role is not updated.
  A suspended workspace binding can still be raised by a stronger claim (documented current behaviour).
