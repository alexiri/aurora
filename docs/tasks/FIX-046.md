# FIX-046: Sidebar alignment corrections from the owner's screenshot — the Workspace selector loses its indent; the narrow-screen drawer opens from the right, under its button (owner, 2026-11-11)

## Problem

Two presentation defects in the shell, both from the owner's 390 px screenshot:

1. **The Workspace selector is indented under the Organization one.** The owner: "the
   Workspaces drop-down doesn't need to be invented under the organization one, the
   hierarchy is clear enough as is" — clarified on this brief's first draft (which had
   read that as "remove the selector"): **"Do NOT delete the workspace selector!!
   Simply don't indent it visually in the menu, have it span the same width as the
   organization selector."** FIX-027 (`docs/14-web-dashboard.md:160-172`, merged
   b028a9b4) renders the Workspace selector "below it and indented":
   `ScopeShellSelector`'s `indent` prop puts `pl-8` on the whole wrapper — caption
   *and* trigger — so WORKSPACE starts ~32 px right of ORGANIZATION and the control is
   narrower than the Organization control. The nesting is already conveyed by order and
   by the captions themselves; the indent invents a hierarchy cue the owner does not
   want and buys a misaligned, harder-to-hit control.
2. **The hamburger button is on the right but the panel opens from the left.** The
   owner: "The three-line hamburger menu is on the right, but it opens the panel on
   the left. This makes no sense, the menu button should be on the right." The
   narrow-screen top bar already places the button at the top-right
   (`app-shell.tsx:458` `justify-between`: logo left, hamburger right), but the drawer
   content is `fixed inset-y-0 left-0 … border-r` (`app-shell.tsx:474-475`) — it
   slides in from the *left*, under the logo, on the opposite side from its trigger.

## Goal

The shell's geometry matches its own cues: the two stacked selectors span the same
width, and the navigation drawer opens on the side its button is on (right). No
behavioural change to either selector, the nav or the tour.

## Decision (D-163, owner 2026-11-11)

(a) Keep FIX-027's stacked pair; express the organization→workspace nesting by **order
and caption, not indentation** — drop the indent so the two selectors span the same
width. (b) The narrow-screen drawer **opens from the right**, under its trigger:
button and panel share a side, like the desktop sidebar's relationship to its content.
