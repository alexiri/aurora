# FIX-288: an approval raised further down a delegation chain reaches the person who asked (FIX-252's open work, 2026-11-28, security)

## Why

D-489 (owner 2026-11-26: approvals "need to travel back to the original request point") was built by FIX-252 for
**one hop**. When a person asks agent A, A delegates to B, and B needs an approval, the card goes to the person's
conversation.

FIX-252's PROGRESS row says: "approvals raised inside a delegated conversation by an onward call are NOT covered yet:
open work". The case is A → B → C, where B, working on A's task, delegates on to C and C needs an approval. C's card is
delivered to the origin of C's task, which is B's task conversation. No person reads that conversation. So the person
who started the chain never learns that something waits for a decision, and the task waits until it expires.

## Decision (Orchestrator, within D-489)

An approval raised in a task travels to the **first conversation in the chain that a person is in**: walk the task's
origin, and the origin's own task origin, until a conversation that isn't a peer task conversation.
- Everything D-489 says applies there: the D-178 redaction, the decide buttons only for someone with `can_approve` in
  the **target** workspace (C's), otherwise Ask an approver / Cancel, the outcome posted back to that conversation, and
  each delegating agent in the chain getting its follow-up turn in its own conversation.
- The walk is bounded by the existing delegation depth limit, and it never crosses into a conversation the chain
  didn't come from.
- If the owner wants the card in every intermediate conversation too, that's a later change; record it as an open
  question.

## Change

- `peer_tasks` (approval delivery): resolve the human origin through the chain, under the lock order written at the
  top of `peer_tasks/service.rs`.
- The approval notice and the outcome notice use that origin.
- The requester whose rights decide the buttons is the person in that origin conversation, the chain's first
  requester. Being the requester grants no right; `can_approve` in C's workspace decides.
- An approval decided anywhere ends the wait for every task in the chain that waits on it, exactly once.
- Update `docs/07` and `docs/10`, and amend C-1167 if its shape changes.

## Tests (each must fail before the fix)

- A → B → C with an approval at C: the card is in the person's conversation, with the right buttons for a person with
  and without `can_approve` in C's workspace.
- Approving posts the outcome there, and B and A each get their follow-up turn.
- Denying or expiring: the same.
- A chain at the depth limit still delivers.
- A delegated conversation whose chain has no human origin (an automation started it): the card goes where FIX-252
  sends it today, the automation's own conversation. Say what that is.
- Delivery twice (bus and sweep) is a single card.
