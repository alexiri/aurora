# FIX-205: The schedule's "When": Once's date sits in the row, and Custom is a preset like the others (owner 2026-11-21)

## Why

The owner, on the schedule form with **Once** chosen: "fix the schedule date picker so it doesn't span the entire width
and so the time and [time zone] are on the same line, like in all the other presets. Also, why the hell does Custom 'need
more controls' that have to be shown separately? All the other options hide and show controls, why is this one any
different?"

- **The date.** `components/automations/schedule-form.tsx` (~:241) renders Once's date as a native
  `<input type="date" className={`${inputClass} w-auto`}>`. `inputClass` starts with `block w-full`, and `w-full` wins
  over `w-auto`, so the input takes the whole row and the flex row wraps: the time and zone drop to a second line. Every
  other preset's controls (`Select … className="w-auto"`, zone `Combobox` `w-72`) share one line.
- **Custom.** P5-REFINE-14 put the raw cron field at the Expert altitude (`Altitude id="auto.cron" level="expert"`,
  `lib/altitude-registry.ts:49`, following D-406's Expert list, "raw cron"). Without the per-person switch, the Custom
  segment is disabled and a line "Custom needs more controls · Show" turns the switch on for the whole dashboard. Every
  other preset just shows its own controls when chosen. D-443 (this fix) makes Custom the same.

## Decision

**D-443** (owner, 2026-11-21): Custom is a preset like the others. Anyone who can edit the schedule can choose it;
choosing it shows the cron field (with its hint and the too-often check), leaving it hides it. The `auto.cron` altitude is
removed, and D-406's Expert list no longer includes the raw cron. The schedule row keeps reading "Custom schedule" with
the raw cron under Technical details (`14 §Automations`, unchanged).
