# P5-UX-11: Accounts for the whole organization (owner, 2026-11-15)

## Goal

Org owners and admins can make an account that belongs to the whole organization through the API.
Starter grants and skill libraries require such accounts, and workspace owners may already give them to
their agents. But **no dashboard path creates one**:
- every connect in the dashboard makes a workspace account (`connect-dialog.tsx` ~302, ~649);
- the organization's **Connect…** asks for a workspace, and lists every workspace of the organization,
  other people's personal ones included, in creation order;
- the organization's account list sends people back to that chooser.

This task adds "connect for the whole organization", the organization's actions on its accounts, and the
**limit** of an organization's account: one of the same three choices as an agent's access, which no
agent in any workspace can exceed (D-332, D-344, D-353).

## Context

- **API.** `connect/service.rs` `authorize_scope` (~165–167) requires an org owner or admin for
  `scope: organization`. `grants/service.rs` (~674–679) accepts an organization's account for agents of
  any workspace of that organization.
- **The limit today.** P5-UX-4 reports `limit` for an organization's account from the connection's
  `access` (`read` → `read`, `write` → `act`) and refuses a choice above it (`above_account_limit`).
  Nothing can express "agents must ask". The gateway enforces `read` on the connection itself (D-240).
- **Dashboard.**
  - The organization's Apps tab: `components/apps/org-apps-list.tsx` (~66–67: the caller's owned
    workspaces, which for an org admin is every workspace).
  - The organization's Accounts tab: P5-UX-4's `AccountsList` over `GET /v1/orgs/{org}/accounts`, with
    no organization actions yet.
  - The journey: `components/apps/connect-journey/*`.
- **Data.** `/v1/me` marks only the caller's own personal workspace. Organization workspace items carry
  no owner of a personal workspace.

## Scope

1. **`personal_of` (C-947).** `GET /v1/orgs/{org}/workspaces` items gain `personal_of?: { id, name }` for
   personal workspaces. OpenAPI and generated types.
2. **The limit (C-990, SEC-131).**
   - Migration: `app_connections.limit_level` (`read`, `ask`, `act`; organization scope only), filled
     from `access`. Take the next free migration number from PROGRESS when you start, and edit that
     line. Move the planned row into `03`'s table; regenerate `spec_schema.json`.
   - The accounts API reports the stored limit; `above_account_limit` (C-980) checks against it,
     including for custom choices (a write under `read`; a write without asking under `ask`).
   - `PATCH /v1/connections/{id}` accepts `limit` for an organization's connection (org owner or admin)
     and keeps `access` in step. The connect route accepts `limit` for `scope: organization` (C-957).
   - **Lowering** the limit lowers, in the same transaction, every agent's access above it, audited
     with `via: "limit"`. `GET /v1/connections/{id}/limit-preview?limit=` lists who would change.
     Raising the limit changes no agent.
   - The policy evaluator does not change.
3. **The journey's organization mode (C-936).**
   - `scope: "organization"`, no agent step.
   - The **Account** step offers an existing organization account of the app ("Use <name>"), which skips
     to Done.
   - The **Access** step sets the account's **limit** instead of an agent's access: the question
     "Agents may at most:" over P5-UX-4's three `ChoiceCards`, with the same names and order, Read only
     preselected.
   - **Done** reads "<App> is connected for <org>. No agent can use it yet." and offers **Give access**
     (P5-UX-4's picker, over agents of the workspaces the person owns, rows "<workspace> · <agent>", own
     and shared workspaces first, each with its access choice within the limit; saved with C-504) and
     **Done**.
4. **Where it starts.**
   - The organization's Accounts tab: **Add › Connect an app or website**, and its empty state.
   - The organization's Apps tab: **Connect** on an app.
   - The organization's setup page for an app: **Connect**.
5. **Where to connect (C-947).** When **Connect** on the organization's Apps tab asks where, it lists:
   1. **For the whole organization**;
   2. the person's own and shared workspaces;
   3. other people's personal workspaces, under a collapsed **Other people's personal workspaces**.
6. **The organization's accounts (C-937).** On the organization's Accounts tab, for org owners and
   admins, a row has:
   - the **limit** control: "Agents may at most: <choice>", opening the three choices. Lowering it shows
     the confirmation from the preview ("3 agents go from Change without asking to Read only") before
     saving;
   - in the menu, in the order of `14` §1: **Give access** (the picker of scope 3), Rename,
     **Reconnect** (the C-842 reconnect, organization scope), Test, **Turn off** or **Turn on**,
     **Remove**.
7. **In a workspace.** The organization's accounts appear in the workspace's Accounts list and in the
   journey's Account step, marked "Shared by <org>" with who manages them (`WhoCan`). A workspace owner
   can give them to their agents within the limit ("Limited by <org>") and cannot change them.
8. **Words.** Remove this task's files from P5-IA-5's vocabulary allow-list.
9. **Tests.**
   - API (SEC-131's list for limits):
     - `personal_of` only on personal workspaces;
     - the migration's fill;
     - a choice above each limit → 422, custom choices included;
     - lowering to Ask before changes turns allowed changes into asked ones and a Change without asking
       level into Ask before changes; lowering to Read only removes changes; each audited;
     - raising changes nobody; a workspace owner cannot change the limit;
     - a gateway test: after lowering to Ask before changes, a change by an agent that had Change without
       asking is held for approval.
   - Vitest: the organization mode's steps; the limit step; the lowering confirmation; the Done picker's
     order and row format; the chooser's order; the organization row's actions by role.
   - Playwright:
     - an org owner connects AWS for the organization with the limit Read only, and gives it to two
       agents in two workspaces; both agents' Access tabs show it as "Shared by <org>", Read only;
     - a workspace owner gives the same account to a third agent, cannot choose more than Read only, and
       sees no Reconnect or Remove;
     - the org owner raises the limit to Ask before changes, after which Ask before changes can be
       chosen and Change without asking cannot.
   - Axe and narrow-route suites, both themes.

## Acceptance criteria

1. An org owner can make an account for the whole organization from the dashboard, set its limit, and
   give it to agents in several workspaces in one flow.
2. The limit is one of the three access choices. No agent's access is above it, at any time: not when it
   is chosen, not after the limit is lowered, not through Always allow.
3. The organization's accounts can be reconnected, limited, turned off and removed from the
   organization's Accounts tab.
4. Workspace owners see and can give the organization's accounts, within the limit, and cannot change
   them.
5. **Connect** leads with "For the whole organization" and folds other people's personal workspaces
   away.
6. Rust and web gates; migration check; a `security-coverage.md` row; axe and narrow-route suites, both
   themes.
