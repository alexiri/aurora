# P5-UX-11: Accounts for the whole organization (owner, 2026-11-15)

## Goal

Org owners and admins can make an account that belongs to the whole organization through the API.
Starter grants and skill libraries require such accounts, and workspace owners may already give them to
their agents. But **no dashboard path creates one**:
- every connect in the dashboard makes a workspace account (`connect-dialog.tsx` ~302, ~649);
- the organization's **Connect…** asks for a workspace, and lists every workspace of the organization,
  other people's personal ones included, in creation order;
- the organization's account list sends people back to that chooser.

This task adds "connect for the whole organization", the organization's actions on its accounts, and the
shared-account limit that goes with them (D-332, D-344).

## Linked requirements

- D-332, D-344, D-352 (no starter-grant option in this flow); contracts **C-936, C-937, C-947**.
- Uses C-22 (connect with `scope: organization`), I-27 (workspace owners can read the organization's
  accounts), C-504 (bulk grant), C-740 (`PATCH access`, here the limit), C-842 (reconnect), C-917, C-980.
- Normative: `14 §Agents, accounts and access` §11 and §2 (shared accounts), `07` (an organization's
  account can be given to agents of any of its workspaces), `04` permission matrix.

## Wave and order

- **Wave 3.** Starts after P5-UX-3 (the journey), P5-UX-4 (the accounts list, the limit rules), P5-UX-9
  (the organization's setup page) and P5-UX-14 (the row's health and Test).

## Context

- **API.** `connect/service.rs` `authorize_scope` (~165–167) requires an org owner or admin for
  `scope: organization`. `grants/service.rs` (~674–679) accepts an organization's account for agents of
  any workspace of that organization.
- **Dashboard.**
  - The organization's Apps tab: `components/apps/org-apps-list.tsx` (~66–67: the caller's owned
    workspaces, which for an org admin is every workspace).
  - The organization's Accounts tab: P5-UX-4's `AccountsList` over `GET /v1/orgs/{org}/accounts`, with
    no organization actions yet.
  - The journey: `components/apps/connect-journey/*`.
- **Data.** `/v1/me` marks only the caller's own personal workspace. Organization workspace items carry
  no owner of a personal workspace.

## Scope

1. **`personal_of` (C-947).** `GET /v1/orgs/{org}/workspaces` items gain `personal_of?: { id, name }` for
   personal workspaces. OpenAPI and generated types.
2. **The journey's organization mode (C-936).**
   - `scope: "organization"`, no agent step.
   - The **Account** step offers an existing organization account of the app ("Use <name>"), which skips
     to Done.
   - The **Access** step sets the account's **limit** instead of an agent's access: "Agents may at most:
     **Read only** (recommended) / **Change things**".
   - **Done** offers **Give agents access now** (the picker over agents of the workspaces the person
     owns, rows "<workspace> · <agent>", own and shared workspaces first, each with its access choice
     within the limit; saved with C-504) and **Done**.
3. **Where it starts.**
   - The organization's Accounts tab: **Add › Connect an app or website**, and its empty state.
   - The organization's Apps tab: **Connect…** on an app.
   - The organization's setup page for an app: **Connect for the organization**.
4. **Where to connect (C-947).** When **Connect…** on the organization's Apps tab asks where, it lists:
   1. **For the whole organization**;
   2. the person's own and shared workspaces;
   3. other people's personal workspaces, under a collapsed **Other people's personal workspaces**.
5. **The organization's accounts (C-937).** On the organization's Accounts tab, for org owners and
   admins, a row has:
   - the **limit** control ("Agents may at most: …"), saved with `PATCH /v1/connections/{id}`;
   - **Reconnect** (the C-842 reconnect, organization scope), **Disable** and **Remove**;
   - **Give access** (the picker of scope 2).
6. **In a workspace.** The organization's accounts appear in the workspace's Accounts list and in the
   journey's Account step, marked "Shared by <org>" with who manages them (`WhoCan`). A workspace owner
   can give them to their agents within the limit and cannot change them.
7. **Words.** Remove this task's files from P5-IA-5's vocabulary allow-list.
8. **Tests.**
   - API: `personal_of` only on personal workspaces.
   - Vitest: the organization mode's steps; the limit step; the Done picker's order and row format; the
     chooser's order; the organization row's actions by role.
   - Playwright:
     - an org owner connects AWS for the organization with the limit Read only, and gives it to two
       agents in two workspaces; both agents' Access tabs show it as "Shared by <org>", Read only;
     - a workspace owner gives the same account to a third agent, cannot choose more than Read only, and
       sees no Reconnect or Remove;
     - the org owner raises the limit, after which Ask before changes can be chosen.
   - Axe and narrow-route suites, both themes.

## Constraints / file ownership

- Owns: the organization mode of `connect-journey`; `org-apps-list.tsx` (the chooser); the
  organization's row actions and limit control in the accounts components; the "Shared by" label; the
  `personal_of` field.
- No change to how access or limits are stored (C-980 stays as P5-UX-4 built it).

## Acceptance criteria

1. An org owner can make an account for the whole organization from the dashboard, set its limit, and
   give it to agents in several workspaces in one flow.
2. The organization's accounts can be reconnected, limited, disabled and removed from the organization's
   Accounts tab.
3. Workspace owners see and can give the organization's accounts, within the limit, and cannot change
   them.
4. **Connect…** leads with "For the whole organization" and folds other people's personal workspaces
   away.
5. Rust and web gates; axe and narrow-route suites, both themes.

## Suggested agent/model and reason

Sonnet (`nextjs-developer`, with one small API field). It extends the journey and the account rows; the
limit control needs care with roles.
